Screw Jam: Girls Puzzle

Existing game-specific policy: effective date and last updated 6 October 2026. Its substantive disclosures are retained below.

1. Scope and contact

This policy explains how information is handled by Screw Jam: Girls Puzzle (the “game”) and its integrated services. The data controller is Serhiy Hryn, a private individual and independent developer. GSV Studio is a project/developer name only and is not a registered company or separate legal entity. For privacy questions or requests, contact gsvstudio115@gmail.com.

2. Game data and accounts

The game saves your level progress, earned Gallery unlocks and sound, music, effects and vibration settings on your device. Temporary gameplay and rescue state is used to operate the current session. The game does not create an in-game user account or require registration or login.

No GSV Studio gameplay server, cloud-save service or independent analytics upload is integrated into this game. The game does not send your saved progress or Gallery to GSV Studio. Your operating system may separately back up this local data, as explained below. Local save data is not encrypted by the game's save system; avoid treating it as a place for sensitive information.

3. Google advertising and privacy services

The game includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising availability depends on the build, configuration, region and consent choices. At this policy's last update, advertising was disabled in the release configuration under preparation. Consent services may still contact Google to check privacy requirements; disabled advertising does not mean that every Google SDK communication is disabled.

When Google Mobile Ads operates, Google's SDK may process the following information:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • Product and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics and performance information about the app and SDK.
  • Device and account-related identifiers, including Android advertising ID, App Set ID and other identifiers where applicable.

Google's documented SDK purposes include advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The information may be transmitted to Google and advertising recipients disclosed through Google's services and applicable privacy messages. GSV Studio does not receive your device's account login details through an in-game account system. The game does not request GPS location permission; approximate location can instead be inferred from an IP address.

UMP checks applicable privacy-message requirements and handles consent choices. A form appears when required by the configured Google service. This does not mean every user will see a form on every launch.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. No independent Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, social-login or mediation SDK is currently integrated.

4. Consent and privacy choices

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent is requested where required for advertising-related storage and processing, including personalized advertising. Available choices depend on the configured message and service. When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, the game's Settings provides access to that form so you can review or change your choices. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

You can also use Android's advertising privacy controls to reset or delete the advertising ID, where supported. These controls do not necessarily stop all service communications or all other identifiers. Declining a rewarded advertisement does not remove the game's clean-play route.

5. Support communications

If you email GSV Studio, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and handle relevant support, privacy or legal matters. Please do not send passwords, payment information or identity documents. The game contains no support form.

6. Retention, deletion and Android backup

Local progress and settings remain until they are replaced or removed. You can remove the current local game data through Android Settings → Apps → Screw Jam: Girls Puzzle → Storage → Clear storage/data. This resets local progress and earned Gallery unlocks.

Uninstalling removes the app's local data, but Android or Google backup services may retain a prior backup and restore it when the app is reinstalled or a device is restored. Reinstallation therefore does not always start a new game. Clearing local storage does not necessarily erase a platform backup; manage backups separately through your device/account backup controls. Ordinary app updates are designed to preserve progress.

We keep support messages for as long as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations, then remove information that is no longer needed. Google controls retention and deletion for information it processes under its policies and available privacy controls. Clearing game storage does not itself delete information already processed by Google.

7. Security and processing locations

Google states that data collected by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using TLS. This statement concerns that SDK's documented transmission and is not a guarantee for every device, email or third-party service. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Google services may process information in countries other than your own. Google's privacy information describes processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards. GSV Studio does not operate a separate international gameplay-data transfer service.

8. Age and privacy rights

This policy does not set a minimum playing age or designate the game as child-directed. The store's audience information and advertising configuration determine the applicable age-related treatment. A parent or guardian with a concern can contact the privacy email above.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us about information GSV Studio holds. For information handled by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google as appropriate. We may need information sufficient to verify a request, but please do not send identification documents unless a specific need has been explained.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. The current policy and its updated date will be available on this page. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required.

Randevu for Android

Randevu section updated: 11 October 2026. Production package: com.gsvstudio.randevu. The game is being prepared for release; availability of purchases, advertising and downloads depends on the build and service configuration.

1. Local stories and downloadable content

Randevu is free to install, with optional purchases and advertising. One character is selected for free; optional purchases can unlock the remaining stories and remove automatic advertisements. The game does not require a separate Randevu account.

Story progress and choices are kept separately for each character. Settings, access records and unlocked gallery information are also stored locally. Character images and voice languages are delivered separately through Google Play Asset Delivery. Downloads require storage and may use chargeable mobile data. Deleting downloaded packs does not intentionally delete character saves, access rights or preserved unlocked gallery images. Changing voice language retains images and retires that character's old voice packs only after successful preparation of the new voice.

No GSV Studio gameplay server or cloud-save service is currently integrated. Android/Google backup may operate separately. Clearing Randevu storage through Android Settings deletes local progress and gallery data; deleting downloadable packs is different. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but platform backup may restore some information. Local saves are not secure storage for sensitive information.

2. Google Play Billing and Unity IAP

Purchases are handled through Google Play Billing using Unity In-App Purchasing. The game processes product identifiers, payment/purchase status, purchase tokens and signed receipts to validate access, acknowledge purchases and restore eligible rights. Verified purchase records are retained locally. The game's own code does not collect payment-card details or Google account passwords. Google processes payment and account information separately. Restoring a purchase does not restore story progress.

The integrated Unity IAP 5.4.4 SDK contains purchase/session reporting to Unity services. This reporting can include installation/session/event identifiers, installation timestamp, package/app/engine versions, device model, operating system, language/locale, country and purchase events, product identifiers, prices, currency and transaction identifiers. Available fields depend on the actual configuration and event. Optional authentication/analytics/external identifiers are not evidence that an in-game account or independent analytics service exists. No separate Unity Authentication or Analytics package is currently configured in Randevu.

Unity's documented purposes and roles vary by activity: it may act as an independent controller or as a processor. When Unity processes data on our behalf, contact the privacy address above so we can handle and route appropriate requests; Unity provides its own privacy channels for processing it controls. Unity IAP privacy information and Unity's Privacy Policy explain its practices. UMP advertising choices do not automatically provide consent for Unity IAP processing.

3. AdMob and UMP

Randevu integrates Google Mobile Ads and UMP. Automatic interstitials are limited to configured story transitions and ending exits, rather than spoken lines or answer selection. Eligible purchases disable automatic advertisements. That does not promise that every SDK or privacy-service communication stops.

When advertising operates, Google's SDK processes IP-derived general location, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and applicable advertising, App Set or device/account-related identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Information can reach Google and recipients disclosed by its services/privacy messages. Advertising-ID processing depends on settings and applicable controls; App Set ID is not used by our own code for advertising personalization. Google documents TLS protection for its advertising SDK transmissions.

UMP checks applicable privacy-message requirements and handles available choices according to region and final configuration. Settings exposes privacy options when required. Android offers advertising-ID controls. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all service communication. No mediation, separate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics service is currently configured in Randevu.

4. Support, retention, security and rights

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to send to respond to the request. Do not send passwords, card details or identity documents without an explained need. Support messages are retained as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations, then unnecessary information is removed.

Local game information remains until overwritten or removed. Google and Unity control retention for information they handle; clearing local game storage or removing downloads does not automatically erase their records. Services may process information outside your country under their applicable policies and safeguards. No system provides absolute security.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to a supervisory authority. Contact gsvstudio115@gmail.com for information held by the developer and assistance routing service requests. We may require enough information to verify a request, but do not send identification documents unless a specific need has been explained.

This policy does not assign Randevu a 13+ rating or establish child-directed status. Actual store audience and advertising configuration determine age-related treatment. The policy will be updated if functions or data practices change, with additional notice or consent where required.
